This Privacy Policy explains what information AntiRaid processes, why it is processed, how it may be stored, and how users can request deletion.

1. Information AntiRaid Processes

Depending on the features enabled on a Discord server, AntiRaid may process:

  • Discord server/guild IDs.
  • Discord channel IDs.
  • Discord user/member IDs.
  • Discord usernames or tags when required for moderation logs.
  • Warning records.
  • Warning reasons.
  • Moderator information.
  • Security-event timestamps.
  • Server configuration.
  • Operational and error information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Detect potential raids.
  • Manage server lockdowns.
  • Provide moderation commands.
  • Maintain warning records.
  • Send configured security logs.
  • Maintain server configuration.
  • Operate and troubleshoot AntiRaid.
  • Protect the service against abuse.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

AntiRaid does not intentionally collect Discord passwords, Discord account tokens, or payment-card information through the bot.

AntiRaid does not sell or rent application data.

4. Storage and Retention

Server configuration and moderation records may be stored so that settings and records can remain available after the bot restarts.

Temporary member-join timestamps may be held in memory for raid detection. They are not intended to be permanent member history.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for functionality, security, operational purposes, or legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

AntiRaid relies on Discord and hosting/infrastructure providers to operate.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect users and the service.

6. Discord API

AntiRaid uses Discord's API and is subject to Discord's applicable developer terms and policies.

Discord may independently process information according to its own privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of information.

To make a privacy or deletion request, contact the AntiRaid application owner through the support/contact method provided on the application profile.

Do not send passwords, Discord tokens, or other sensitive credentials when making a request.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information processed by AntiRaid.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Deletion

If you remove AntiRaid from your server, you may request deletion of stored server-related information where applicable.

Deletion requests may be subject to legal, security, or operational retention requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the AntiRaid application owner through the support/contact method provided on the AntiRaid application profile.
